Born in January, more often Olympians

Among Olympians born since 1950, people born early in the year are more numerous than births in their country would suggest, and those born late in the year less numerous. This is the relative age effect: in youth categories, a child born in January can be almost a year ahead physically of a teammate born in December, and is more often spotted. Each Olympian is compared here with actual births in their country, in their year of birth.

By country

Same indicator, countries with at least 800 Olympians in the sample.

Pale bars are not significant. The indicator assumes that youth age categories follow the calendar year, which is the most common case. In Japan, the sporting year starts in April: people born in January-March are the youngest in their category, and they are not over-represented among Olympians (0.91, interval 0.82 – 1.01). This is consistent with the relative age explanation. In England, youth football categories are based on age on 31 August: for these sports, the indicator measures the effect poorly.

Method and limits

  • Olympians: Wikidata, every person with an Olympedia identifier (166,517 people), extracted on 18/11/2026, public domain (CC0). We keep those with a single date of birth; dates on 1 January (6,967) are set aside, because Wikidata often stores dates known only by year there, and January is rescaled to 31 days.
  • Country: country for sport if unique, otherwise citizenship if unique (Wikidata).
  • Births by month: Eurostat, demo_fmonth (CC BY 4.0 licence) for European countries, and UNdata, Demographic Statistics Database, United Nations Statistics Division for 18 other countries. Each Olympian is compared with the distribution of births in their country in their year of birth, or the nearest available year within 10 years (same year for 74.4 % of them, average gap 1.3 year).
  • Sample: 58,682 Olympians born since 1950 in 66 countries (35,830 compared with Eurostat data, 22,852 with UN data). Countries without usable monthly statistics, including China, Russia before 2005 and the USSR, are not included.
  • 95 % confidence intervals computed with a Poisson approximation on the ratio of the two quarters.
  • The sport is the one recorded in Wikidata; it is missing for some Olympians. People born in the 2000s are mostly athletes from 2020-2024, a younger sample.
