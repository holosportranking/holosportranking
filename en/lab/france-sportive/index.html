Methodological notes

  • Figures from INJEP's full 2023 census, all licences included, including those outside the geocoding scope or not allocated. They therefore cannot be added directly to the commune rates shown above.
  • We count licences, not people: according to INJEP, the same person can hold several licences, in one federation or in several. A pupil licensed with UNSS and with a football club counts twice. This is why the two scopes must not be added together to measure a number of athletes.
  • The primary and secondary school federations (USEP and UNSS) are geocoded at the address of the school or club, not that of the member. Their figures by commune or département therefore describe places of practice, not places of residence: they are not mapped.
  • The Fédération des clubs de la défense, the Fédération sportive de la Police nationale and the FF Maccabi are only allocated at département level.
  • Change 2019-2023: federations approved after 2019 (omnisport clubs, UCPA) are counted in 2023 but not in 2019. The decline of affinity federations remains clear even without them.
  • The share of young people refers to licences for ages 5 to 19.

Method and sources

  • Licences: licence and club census, INJEP, 2023 edition, Licence Ouverte. Each licence is attached to the commune of the address geocoded by Insee. A person can hold several licences: we count licences, not participants.
  • Scope: as in Insee's regional studies, only single-sport federations, Olympic and non-Olympic, are kept. Multi-sport federations are left out: affinity federations (UFOLEP, gymnastique volontaire, FSGT, Sports pour tous…), school and university federations (UNSS, UGSEL, USEP…) and disability sport. This limits double licences, but also removes participants licensed only in those federations.
  • Licences outside the geocoding scope or not allocated are also left out. Mayotte is outside the scope of the census. The number of clubs and establishments covers all federations.
  • Paris, Lyon and Marseille combine their arrondissements.
  • Facilities: Data ES, Ministry of Sport, Licence Ouverte 2.0, extracted on 18/11/2026.
  • Population: Insee legal populations via the API Géo. Boundaries: IGN Admin Express, Licence Ouverte. Ranks cover the communes with 5,000 inhabitants or more.
